# Contribution Guidelines

Thanks for helping improve this list. Telugu resources in any field are welcome.

## What Belongs Here

- The resource is about Telugu, or has real Telugu support that is worth pointing out.
- It is live, usable and of good quality.
- It is not already listed.
- You would recommend it to a friend. Lists work because they are selective.

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Pirated books, films, music or scraped copies of paid content.
- Pages that exist only for advertising or SEO.
- Abandoned student projects with no documentation or results.
- City or regional associations. Community lists only established national bodies.

## Adding an Entry

1. Pick the most specific section, and add your entry at the end of it.
2. Use this format:

   ```markdown
   - [Name](https://example.com/) - Short description ending with a period.
   ```

3. Start the description with a capital letter, and describe what the resource is. Avoid marketing language.
4. Mention a non-commercial license or a paywall in the description, when one applies.
5. Link directly to the resource, without tracking parameters.
6. Open one pull request per addition, with a title such as `Add Name`.

## Updating or Removing an Entry

If a link is dead, has moved or no longer deserves a place here, please open a pull request or an issue that says why.
